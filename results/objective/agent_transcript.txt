- **32 ms and 16 ms:** the gain is confirmed. Every new seed beats every old seed.

- **Trade-off:** STOI rises slightly at every budget. SI-SDR drops, by about half a dB at the two smaller budgets.

**What worked and what didn't:**
- **Weight:** the suggested 5–50 range was too low. PESQ kept rising up to at least 400. One seed at 1000 gave 2.777, so the best weight may be higher, but that's unconfirmed.

- **8 ms:** at 10k steps the spectral loss gave no gain at any weight. Only with 30k steps did it help. A one-seed run with 30k steps and no spectral loss gave 2.422, which suggests the gain needs both changes together.

**Failures:**
